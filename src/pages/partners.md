Smetase helps Nigerian students find programmes abroad that fit their degree and budget, with every fee checked against the university's own course page. Once a student knows what they want, they need someone experienced to help them apply. That is where partners come in.

## Education agents

You know admissions and visas. We bring you students who already have a programme in mind, with their budget, grades and plan in front of you. You advise them in the Smetase chat, so you never start from a blank conversation.

## Agencies

Bring your team. Each of your advisors gets their own account and sees only the students assigned to them, so your agency can take on more students without losing track of who is handling whom.

## Universities and recruitment teams

If you recruit students from Nigeria, tell us about your programmes. We only show course details that we can check against your own published pages, so students see fees and entry requirements they can trust.

## How we work with partners

- **Everything stays in one chat.** Students and advisors talk inside Smetase, so nothing gets lost and the student always has one place to go.
- **Terms in writing first.** We agree how we work together, and any fees, in writing before you take on a student.
- **Students' data is protected.** Partners see only the students assigned to them, as our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) explains.

## Get in touch

Email [partners@smetase.com](mailto:partners@smetase.com?subject=Partnering%20with%20Smetase) with your name, your company if you have one, and the countries you work with. We will reply within a few days.
